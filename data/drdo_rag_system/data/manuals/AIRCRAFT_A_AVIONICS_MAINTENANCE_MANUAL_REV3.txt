SECTION 1: AVIONICS OVERVIEW
The avionics suite contains navigation, communication, display, monitoring, and data-processing subsystems.

SECTION 2: PRE-MAINTENANCE CHECK
Confirm aircraft identification and maintenance authorization. Record the reported symptom before performing diagnostic checks.

SECTION 3: FAULT REVIEW
Review historical maintenance records and applicable technical bulletins before replacing a component. Verify connector condition, power availability, and relevant built-in test indications.

SECTION 4: COMPONENT REPLACEMENT RECORD
When an authorized component replacement is performed, record the component identifier, previous component status, replacement date, and applicable maintenance reference.
